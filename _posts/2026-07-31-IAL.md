The International Association for Lichenology congress happened in Trieste in the last week of July. It has been 10 years since the last in-person IAL! Gulnara has presented her posdoc research on Xanthoria omics and secreted proteins as a keynote talk on the final day of the conference.

It was an incredible conference! Great to meet so many new people and re-connect with old friends!
